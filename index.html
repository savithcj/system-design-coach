    Board as Mermaid

    
  
    Event log
    
  
    
  
    
    


    

    Arrow keys move this box; hold Shift to move further. Enter or F2 renames it, C connects it to another box, Control or Command C and V copy and paste it, Delete removes it. N adds or edits a note. E moves to its arrows; there, Enter or F2 labels an arrow, the arrow keys switch arrows, Delete removes one and Escape returns to the box. Control or Command Z undoes, with Shift redoes.
